Types of Machine Learning

Definition: The model is trained using labeled data — meaning every example includes both the input and the correct answer.

Main characteristics: Requires a labeled dataset; the model learns by comparing its predictions against known correct answers and correcting its errors.

Types of problems it solves:

  • Classification: assigns data into categories, such as detecting spam emails or recognizing objects in photos.
  • Regression: predicts numeric values, such as house prices or temperature.

Definition: The model receives data with no labels or correct answers, and must find hidden structure on its own.

Main characteristics: No labeled data is required; the model identifies similarities and groups or patterns among the examples.

Types of problems it solves:

  • Clustering: groups similar items together, such as segmenting types of customers.
  • Dimensionality Reduction: simplifies large datasets by keeping only their most relevant features.

Definition: An agent learns by interacting with an environment, taking actions, and receiving rewards or penalties based on the outcome.

Main characteristics: Learning happens through trial and error, guided by a reward signal rather than labeled examples.

Types of problems it solves:

  • Sequential decision-making problems, such as self-driving cars, robotics control, and game-playing agents like chess or Go.
