K-Nearest Neighbors — Application

The Problem

Coaches and cycling apps want an easy way to know a rider's skill level. This page uses a K-Nearest Neighbors model to guess a cyclist's level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) based on the data from one of their rides.

Input Data (X)
  • Distance ridden (km)
  • Total time (minutes)
  • Elevation gain (m)
  • Average heart rate (bpm)
  • Average cadence (rpm)
