What this means

At {{ accuracy }}% accuracy, this model performs well for a 3-class problem, where guessing at random would only get it right about 33% of the time.

The confusion matrix shows the model mixes up neighboring levels the most: Beginner and Intermediate cyclists get confused with each other more than any other pair, and the same happens between Intermediate and Advanced. Notably, the model almost never confuses Beginner with Advanced directly, which makes sense — those two groups are the most different in terms of distance, elevation gain, and pace. This pattern is typical of ordered categories: mistakes tend to happen between "next-door" levels, not between opposite extremes.
