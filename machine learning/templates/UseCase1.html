Use Case 1: Spam Email Detection

Name of the use case: Spam Email Detection

Problem context: Email services receive many unwanted or harmful messages every day. It is hard for a person to check every email manually.

Data that could be used: Thousands of past emails, marked as "spam" or "not spam", including the text, subject, and sender.

What the model should predict: Whether a new email is spam or not spam.

Type of Machine Learning applied: Supervised Learning (Classification).

Potential benefit: Users save time and avoid harmful or annoying messages, without checking every email by hand.
